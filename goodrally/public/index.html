How it works

Five minutes a day. That's it.

Most people have heard about AI but never found a reason to use it. We give you one good reason a day, about something you actually care about: your health.

1

A message each morning

By text, email or WhatsApp, whichever you prefer. One small thing to try, with the exact words to type or say.

2

Try it your way

Use a free app like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Type, or just talk out loud; day one shows you how.

3

Stuck? Just reply

A real person reads your replies. Missed a day? No problem. The club waits for you.

Our promise

AI is your helper. Never your doctor.

  • Every day includes a reminder of what AI is good at, and where to be careful.
  • We never ask for your medical records, and we teach you what not to share.
  • Nothing we suggest replaces advice from your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. It helps you get more out of them.
  • Plain language, big type, no jargon. Bring your grandkids if you like, but you won't need them.

Questions

Good questions.

I'm not good with technology. Is this for me?

Yes, this is exactly who it's for. Day one walks you through getting set up, step by step, and you can talk to the AI out loud instead of typing. If you get stuck, just reply to our message and a real person will help.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. Everything works with the free versions of popular AI apps on a phone, tablet or computer you already have.

Is it safe to use AI for health questions?

AI can be a wonderful helper for getting organised, understanding words and preparing questions, but it can also be wrong. That's why we teach you how to double-check it, what never to share, and why your doctor always has the final say.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing bad happens. Pick up where you left off: the club goes at your pace, not ours.

Can I sign up for my mum or dad?

Of course. Tick the box on the form and add their details. Many people do the 30 days together with a parent, which is a lovely way to spend five minutes a day.
