The fine print (it's short)

Hunt terms

What you're paying for

A one-time fee buys a hunt of the length you choose (7, 30 or 60 days). During that window we search secondhand and retail sources every day for the item you described, and send you matching listings via WhatsApp. It is not a subscription and never auto-renews.

What we don't do

  • We don't buy, hold, pick up, inspect or ship items. You buy directly from the seller, on the seller's terms.
  • We aren't a party to your purchase and can't guarantee a seller's honesty. Use each platform's normal safety practices — meet in public, don't pay strangers with irreversible methods.
  • We never send you listings above your max price.

The 24-hour feasibility check

After you pay, we review your hunt within 24 hours. If we judge it unfindable — consumables, one-of-a-kind items, things with no resale market — we tell you and refund 100% before the hunt clock starts. Your hunt window begins only once we confirm.

Find it or it's free

You get a 100% refund if:

  • your hunt window ends without us sending you a listing that matches your brief and budget; or
  • the listing we send is gone before you reasonably could act on it, or is materially not as described — and we can't find you a replacement before your window ends.

We don't refund for change of mind once we've sent a matching listing, or if you find the item yourself after the hunt has started. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

Your details

We use your phone number to message you about your hunt, and your email for receipts. We don't sell your data or add you to marketing lists. Photos you upload are used only to search for your item; if you're happy for us to feature a found item on our site, we'll ask first.

Things we won't hunt

Anything illegal, recalled, age-restricted, weapons, animals, prescription drugs, or anything we'd feel weird about. We'll decline and refund.

Questions? Just reply to us on WhatsApp.
