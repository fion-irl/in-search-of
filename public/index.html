FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you buy the item for me?

No — we find it, you buy it. We send you the listing link the moment we've confirmed it matches, and you deal with the seller directly. That keeps it fast and means you're never paying a markup.

Is it really a full refund if you don't find it?

Yes. 100%. If your hunt window ends with no match within your budget, we refund the whole fee. Same if the listing we send is gone before you can act, or turns out to be materially not as described. The only exception is changing your mind once we've found it.

Why WhatsApp?

Because good listings sell in hours. WhatsApp means you see "found it!" instantly, with the link, and can reply to us straight away if something's off.

How often do you check?

Every day of your hunt, across Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, OfferUp, Poshmark, Mercari, eBay, Depop, Google Shopping, Reddit and wherever else your item tends to show up. A human reviews every match before it reaches you.

Will you ever send me something over my budget?

Never. Your max price is a hard filter. If it's a dollar over, you won't hear about it.

What if I find it myself first?

Amazing — tell us and we'll close the hunt. (Fees for hunts already underway aren't refundable in that case, but we'll happily count it as a win.)

Where do you hunt?

We're based in the San Francisco Bay Area, so local pickup finds are our specialty — but if you're happy with shipping, we hunt nationwide.
